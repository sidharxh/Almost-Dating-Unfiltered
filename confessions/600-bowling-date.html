₹600, Two Coffees, and a Terrible Bowling Score

Confession #006 · Fictional story · For adults 18+

Posted by u/BrokeButFlirting · Dating Stories

I wanted to impress him.

Unfortunately, my bank account had developed a strong personal commitment to realism.

We were both tired of first dates that felt like job interviews, so I suggested bowling. We agreed on a budget beforehand, and the plan was simple: play a game, grab coffee, and let the loser choose the next snack.

My total spending for the evening was about ₹600.

He arrived looking effortlessly good in a plain T-shirt and jeans. I had spent twenty minutes deciding what to wear and then chose the same outfit I always wore.

My first bowling attempt rolled directly into the gutter.

He laughed.

Then he threw his own ball into the wrong lane.

After that, we stopped trying to look cool. We invented ridiculous victory dances, celebrated accidental pins, and accused the scorekeeper of discriminating against people with excellent personalities.

By the time we sat down for coffee, the awkwardness had completely disappeared.

We talked about work, money, and our plans for the next few years. He was saving for a better laptop. I was helping at home and trying to build an emergency fund.

I admitted that I sometimes felt embarrassed suggesting inexpensive dates because dating apps made it seem as if everyone else was spending their weekends at rooftop bars.

He looked genuinely confused.

"Why would I want you to spend money you don't have just to impress me?"

That sentence was more attractive than anything he could have said about my appearance.

After coffee, he asked whether I'd like to meet again. I said yes.

We agreed to visit a bookstore the following weekend and pick terrible books for each other.

There was no expensive dinner, no dramatic gesture, and no attempt to pretend we were wealthier than we were.

Just two people who enjoyed each other's company.
