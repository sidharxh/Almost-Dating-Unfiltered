We Cooked Dinner Together. I Was Not Prepared for the Chemistry.

Confession #003 · Fictional story · For adults 18+

Posted by u/PaneerAndProblems · Dating & Relationships

I'm 27, and this happened on my second date with a guy I met on Hinge.

Our first date had been at a small dosa place. It was fun, but restaurant bills were getting ridiculous, so I suggested we cook something together.

We planned a simple dinner: paneer wraps, cucumber salad, and ice cream from the neighbourhood grocery store.

Nothing fancy.

He arrived carrying groceries and a speaker. I had prepared a playlist that I was convinced would make me seem effortlessly cool.

Within ten minutes, I had cried while cutting onions, he had confused two spices, and the smoke alarm had become an active participant in our evening.

We were laughing so much that neither of us could finish a sentence.

Eventually, dinner was ready. We sat at my tiny dining table, eating slightly over-salted paneer wraps while discussing childhood crushes and the embarrassing things we'd done to impress people.

Then he asked me a question.

"What makes you feel close to someone?"

I expected something generic about trust or communication.

Instead, I told him that I liked when someone remembered small things I'd mentioned casually. When they noticed I was tired. When they could sit beside me without expecting me to entertain them constantly.

He listened.

A few minutes later, he said, "Can I kiss you?"

I was nervous, but I wanted to. So I said yes.

The kiss was gentle at first. Then we both started smiling, which made it difficult to continue being remotely serious.

He pulled back and said, "I think the paneer was better than the sauce."

I told him he was banned from my kitchen.

We spent the rest of the evening talking and kissing occasionally, without either of us feeling the need to rush things. Later, he checked that I was comfortable with the plan for him to head home.

The next morning, he sent me a photograph of the disastrous chopping board.

Caption: Michelin-starred experience.

We're still seeing each other.
